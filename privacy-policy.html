Privacy Policy

App: Swiss Sapphire (Android) · Developer: Shahzad Riaz · Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Swiss Sapphire wallpaper app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We are committed to protecting your privacy in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Who we are

The App is published by Shahzad Riaz, who is the data controller. Contact: ssmalik8877@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

  • Favourites and settings – wallpapers you save as favourites and your app preferences are stored only on your device.
  • Technical data – when the App loads wallpapers, your device's IP address, device model, Android version and app version may be processed by our image providers and service providers to deliver content.
  • Diagnostics (if enabled) – anonymous crash reports and usage statistics may be collected (for example via Google Firebase) to fix bugs and improve the App.
  • Advertising (if the App shows ads) – ad partners such as Google AdMob may collect your device advertising ID and approximate (IP-based) location to show and measure ads.

3. Permissions

  • Set wallpaper – to apply the wallpaper you choose to your home and/or lock screen.
  • Internet / network state – to load wallpapers.
  • Storage / media (older Android versions only) – to save a wallpaper to your gallery when you tap download.
  • Notifications (optional) – to tell you about new wallpapers, only if you allow it.

4. Third-party services

Wallpaper images are provided through third-party APIs. When you browse, requests are sent to these services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Pixabay – pixabay.com/service/privacy
  • Pinterest – policy.pinterest.com/privacy-policy
  • Google Play Services / Firebase / AdMob (where used) – policies.google.com/privacy

All images remain the property of their respective owners and are shown under the licences of those platforms.

5. How we use information & legal basis

  • To provide and display wallpapers (legitimate interests / performance of the service).
  • To fix crashes and improve the App (legitimate interests).
  • To show personalised ads only where you have given consent; you may otherwise see non-personalised ads (consent).

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Data retention

Data stored on your device is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Crash and analytics data held by service providers is kept for no longer than 14 months. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

7. Data security

All network traffic from the App uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. You can withdraw ad consent at any time from the App's settings or via Android Settings → Google → Ads. To request deletion see our Data Deletion page. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
