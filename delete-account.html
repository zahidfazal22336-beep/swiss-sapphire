Delete your data – Swiss Sapphire

Developer: Shahzad Riaz · Applies to the Swiss Sapphire Android app

Swiss Sapphire does not require you to create an account. Most of your data (favourites, settings) is stored only on your device. You can delete your data in either of the ways below.

Option 1 – Delete data on your device (instant)

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Swiss Sapphire.
  2. Tap Storage & cache → Clear storage, or simply uninstall the App.

This permanently removes your favourites, settings and cached wallpapers.

Option 2 – Request deletion of any data we or our service providers hold

Send us an email with the subject "Swiss Sapphire – Data Deletion Request". If possible, include your device model and the approximate date you used the App.

Email deletion request

We will confirm and complete your request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Favourites, preferences and cached images stored by the App.
  • Any emails or support messages you sent us.
  • Crash/analytics records linked to your app instance, where they can be identified.

What may be kept

  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you.
  • Records we must keep by law (if any), for the period the law requires.

Advertising data is controlled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
